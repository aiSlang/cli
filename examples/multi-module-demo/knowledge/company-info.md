# Acme Demo Company — Customer Support Reference

This document is the source of truth the support agent draws on
when answering customer questions. Drop additional `.md` files in
this directory to expand the agent's knowledge base.

## Returns

We accept returns within **30 days from purchase**. The product
must be in its **original packaging** with all accessories
included. Refunds are processed within 5 business days of receipt.

## Account & Password

To reset your password, visit the **account settings page** and
click "Reset password". A reset link will be sent to your
registered email address. The link expires in 1 hour.

## Payment Methods

We accept **Visa**, **Mastercard**, **American Express**, and
**PayPal**. Bank transfer is available for orders over $500.

## Company Location

Acme Demo Company is headquartered in **Berlin, Germany**, with
additional offices in San Francisco and Tokyo.

## Contact Support

For account, billing, or product questions, email
**support@example.com** or open a ticket from the help page at
acme.example/help. Average response time is under 4 business hours.
