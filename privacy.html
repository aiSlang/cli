Privacy Policy

The aiSlang MCP server runs on your machine, started by your MCP client. It reads and writes the .ais files and the local Docker stacks you point it at, and talks to the LiteLLM proxy configured by AISLANG_LITELLM_URL (default http://127.0.0.1:4000). It sends no telemetry, keeps no logs outside your project directory, and holds no provider credentials — those live in your LiteLLM configuration. Prompts and tool arguments are forwarded only to the model endpoints your stack defines. Contact: support@aislang.org.
